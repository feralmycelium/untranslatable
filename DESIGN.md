# The manuscript is the space

Mode: Experience, with a small Operate layer while drawing.
Keep cream #f0e8d9, ink #282429, cobalt #2b4587, red #a74343.
No frames around artworks and no dashboard. A large continuous paper surface
is the first viewport. Its marks carry the visual identity. Quiet serif
titles, readable small sans-serif controls, 44px touch targets.

The ten works remain recognisable islands in the shared field. Navigation
reveals adjacency and distance; creation uses the same seeded pen gestures.
Contributions enter as traces of the same hand rather than arbitrary uploads.

Top corners: title/home and About. Bottom: Explore/Make switch, zoom and pause.
Contextual making controls show only during a draft. A compact index and
overview offer orientation. Every action remains available through buttons
or keyboard, never an undisclosed gesture alone.

Opening scene: the original wandering passage fills the screen in generous
detail. Neighbouring ink enters as you wander. A brief hint explains drag
and zoom. One small invitation, Make a mark, is the only primary action.

Motion purpose: slow ink motion makes the art alive; camera movement reveals
space. Direct pointer tracking is immediate. Guided camera travel ~650ms,
interruptible at pointer-down. Reduced motion disables travel and starts ink
paused. Pause, focus, draft, error and successful shared save are explicit.

Art reference: public/art/original.svg and the collection in public/asemic.js.
Preserve the accepted handwriting when extending this world.
