# Artwork reuse

The seed artwork and marks explicitly published into this instance are made
available under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Attribute reused work to “Untranslatable contributors”, link to the source
instance and the license, and indicate changes. Individual contributors retain
their rights; publishing a mark grants reuse under these terms. Drafts that
have not been published are not included in the shared world or its export.
